The runtime package is `public/data/rapperswil-uznach`. OSM geometry and attributes are open-mapping observations, not authoritative railway operational records. The rendered spline, inferred lateral placement, vertical profile, ballast, terrain interpolation/triangles and catenary are derived. Signal aspects, block authority, dynamics, controller, scoring and energy are simulated.

No raw national dataset is committed. Cached requests live below ignored `data/raw/` and `data/intermediate/`. Official datasets have different acquisition/update dates, so this is an open-data geographic reconstruction, not a contemporaneous survey or “digital twin.”

Building surface classification and grounding are derived from swissBUILDINGS3D semantics and the visual terrain. Roof colours are derived from SWISSIMAGE; wall colours are synthetic and deterministic. Eight scenario signals beyond the mapped coverage are explicitly `SIMULATED_SCENARIO`. Station locations/platforms are OSM; targets, nameboards and marker posts are derived. The S17 timetable comes from official static feed `GTFS_FP2026_20260822.zip`; player arrival, dwell, doors and score are simulated. The local minimap uses the distributed OSM vectors, while its train marker is simulated on the mapped route.

Surrounding rail ways, shared nodes, mapped switch locations and source IDs are OpenStreetMap data. Graph splitting, player/AI paths, platform-edge association, signal-section association and rendered trackwork are derived. AI timetable calls for 11448, 11648 and 2025 are official static GTFS data for 22 August 2026. Their intermediate positions, motion, occupancy, routes, switch states and every signal aspect are simulated.
