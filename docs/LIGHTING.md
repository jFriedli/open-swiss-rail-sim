# Lighting

The geographic sun drives one directional light; a hemisphere light supplies restrained sky/ground fill. Tone-mapping exposure changes continuously with daylight and cloud cover. Night remains dark but driveable through low ambient contribution, emissive signals, a modest player headlight and synthetic platform lights.

Station lamps are generated from real mapped platform geometry and fade on across twilight. Their exact positions are synthetic. Signals retain simulated aspects; only emissive prominence changes with ambient light. No point light is created for every building window or station lamp.
