# Physics

The generic 290 t Swiss electric passenger consist has 4 MW power, 240 kN maximum tractive effort, 0.9 m/s² full service braking and 1.25 m/s² emergency braking. Traction is force-limited at low speed and power-limited above it. Resistance uses `5000 + 180v + 8v²` newtons. Gradient force is `m g gradient/1000`; route gradient comes from smoothed elevation geometry. Positive wheel work is accumulated as traction energy; 25% of braking work is displayed as approximate regeneration. These are transparent simulation assumptions, not specifications for an SBB vehicle.
